A Fluid–Structure‑Coupled Physics‑Informed Neural Operator for Intraventricular Vector Flow Mapping from Color Doppler Echocardiography

        
With Dr. Fangxu Xing (Harvard Medical School). A dual‑PINO architecture that couples fluid and structural dynamics to reconstruct intraventricular vector flow non‑invasively from clinical color Doppler. First‑author abstract submitted to SPIE Medical Imaging 2027.

Predicting Pancreatic Cancer Metastasis
