          Harvard Medical School / MGH · Submitted to SPIE Medical Imaging 2027 · First author
          01 · MEDICAL IMAGING
        

        
A Fluid–Structure-Coupled Physics-Informed Neural Operator for Intraventricular Vector Flow Mapping from Color Doppler Echocardiography

        
Color Doppler only measures blood velocity along the ultrasound beam. Existing methods rebuild the full flow field by fitting a new network to every scan, which takes minutes. This work trains coupled fluid and wall-motion neural operators once, then reconstructs an unseen case in a single ~31 ms pass while enforcing the physics and inferring the moving heart wall instead of importing it.

        

          
Speed
~31 ms
per case, ~4 orders of magnitude faster

          
In-vivo velocity
R² 0.719
held-out exams

          
Cross-beam velocity
R² 0.944
vs 0.54 per-case fitting

          
Pressure
R² 0.998
Taylor–Green, vs 0.92

        

        
Armaan Gugnani, Shiyi Wang, Ruochen Sun, Fangxu Xing · Department of Radiology

SMG Signals

      
A quant system for my DECA team. It scans the US market daily, scores trades across four strategies, sizes risk to maximize the odds of hitting our goal, and emails verified trade tickets five times a trading day.
