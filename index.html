The four sleeves

          
    
            
  • Core momentum. Ranks the market on 52‑week‑high proximity and volume surge, now optionally an ML ridge blend of five features.
    • 
            
  • Earnings. Names with a history of big report reactions: buy the close before, sell the reaction day unless it breaks out.
    • 
            
  • Fade. Short a +60% first‑day blow‑off that's already fading, or the first red day after a parabolic run.
    • 
            
  • Runner. Day 1–2 of a +20% move: liquid, no recent dilution filing, never a SPAC.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Sizing · goal mode

          
Every day it computes the closed‑form probability of reaching the equity goal by the deadline, then picks the risk level that maximizes it. Stickiness and a lock‑at‑goal rule keep it from thrashing, and per‑level μ and σ come from a two‑year backtest.

          
P equals Phi of: n times mu, minus the natural log of G over x, all divided by sigma times the square root of n.P = Φ( (n·μ − ln(G/x)) / (σ√n) )

          
Risk

          
Hard −12% stops that never loosen, 10‑day‑low exits, pyramid adds, profit locks, and a fat‑tailed, correlated Monte Carlo that models short‑squeeze convexity.

        

        

          
What the research found

          
The obvious edge was fake. Momentum "runners" looked profitable until survivorship bias and reverse‑split errors in the data were corrected, and then they turned negative. The strongest edge in a two‑year event study was the opposite trade: fading parabolic blow‑offs.

          
2‑year backtest harness · IC, top‑k and quintile evaluation · 331 strategy variants tested · adversarial model‑critic agent
